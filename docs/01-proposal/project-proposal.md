## Problem Statement
The current manual hostel allocation and booking process at USTED causes significant stress, delays, and frustration for students. Manual records are prone to misplacement, and it is highly difficult for students to track the live availability of rooms or check the real-time status of lodged complaints.

## Proposed Solution
A web-based student hostel management system featuring a real-time booking engine, live room discovery filters, automated digital receipt verification, and an integrated SMS gateway to streamline communications and room allocations transparently.
